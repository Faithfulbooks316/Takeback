Keep your install healthy

Install Termux from a trusted source and keep Termux and its add-ons from the same source. Do not use sudo or install Debian/Ubuntu packages into Termux; it is not a rooted Linux distribution. Android may stop long-running jobs when battery optimization is enabled.
